01 About

Finance, technology and analytics —
treated as one discipline.

I'm a Financial Technology student at Bahrain Polytechnic, drawn to the point where finance, technology and analytics meet. Most of what I know came from building things that had to actually work — stores with real customers, models with real error, projects with real deadlines.

My day-to-day sits in data analysis, visualization, machine-learning fundamentals and digital transformation projects. Alongside my degree I completed a Data Science bootcamp at General Assembly, where I built three end-to-end projects including a classification capstone on the Adult Census dataset.

Before that, five years of e-commerce taught me the commercial half of the job: pricing, customer behaviour, campaign performance and the difference between a number and a decision. That's the perspective I bring to an analytics team.
